what sunday looks like

  1. about an hour

    Acoustic, upbeat music and a message from the Bible, in 65 minutes.

    Come in jeans. Donuts and coffee are waiting every week.

  3. kids have their own spot

    Sunnyside Kids is for babies through 5th grade. Check-in opens at 9:40 in the school library.
