# Credits

Cornerstone Bible Fellowship and Westfield are fictional; this folder is a public demo of the church-design skill's Classic Confident direction.

## Photos

All photos are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) and hotlinked from `images.unsplash.com`. They stand in for the church's own photography, which should replace them. The homepage photos show no children or students; the Ministries page uses one stock photo of children for Cornerstone Kids, which should be the first photo replaced, with the church's own consented photos of kids (favor shots where faces are not the focus).

## Marks

`mark.svg` is an original mark drawn for this demo: a squared letter C laid in dressed stone, resting on a larger cornerstone.

The twelve ministry marks (blocks, steps, arch, pillar, table, house, cairn, altar, shelter, bowl, sound, cup) are an original family drawn for this demo from the same dressed-stone block, on a 24px grid at a 2px stroke. They are defined as SVG symbols in `ministries.html`.
