# Page types

## What applies everywhere, and what changes per page

**Sitewide (every page, never varies):**
- Brand constants: fonts, colors, radius family and photo shape, button shape, overlay treatment, section rhythm (`foundations.md`)
- Spacing, margins, and balance rules (`foundations.md`)
- The church's mark and one icon set (`iconography.md`)
- Header, navigation, and footer, with Give always reachable (`modules.md`)
- Accessibility, writing voice, and anti-patterns

**Per page type (this file):** each page has one job. That job decides the hero, how much depth the page carries, and the section order. Pages share modules; they do not share layouts.

**Depth rule:** the homepage introduces and links. Inner pages hold the detail. A topic appears in full on exactly one page and is summarized (with a link) everywhere else.

**Hero rule:** the homepage gets the largest hero. Inner pages use a shorter hero (about 45 to 60% of the viewport height, or a typographic header with a photo below) so the content starts sooner.

## Homepage

- **Job:** welcome a stranger and send them one step deeper.
- **Hero:** full-bleed photo or video, headline bottom-left (or top on a portrait phone crop), and the direction's when-and-where element with times and address. Playful Modern may use its type-led hero instead.
- **Sections:** first Sunday promises → Scripture or mission moment → find your place index → latest message → coming up (3) → one mission or community feature → give and prayer.
- **Limits:** 6 to 8 sections, about 40 words of body copy per section. See `commands/design.md`.

## Plan a Visit ("I'm new", "Visit", "Everything Sunday")

- **Job:** remove every worry before Sunday.
- **Hero:** short photo hero of the front door or lobby with the times and address in it.
- **Sections:** what to expect (full Q&A grid: length, music, dress, kids check-in, parking, entrance, coffee, communion, accessibility) → kids ministry detail with a pre-register action → map and directions → a short welcome from a pastor (photo, two or three sentences) → "Tell us you're coming" form or contact.
- **Depth:** this is the one page that answers everything about Sunday.

## Sermons ("Watch", "Media")

- **Job:** watch the latest message or find an older one.
- **Hero:** the latest sermon as the hero (large video or thumbnail, title, passage, speaker, date, notes).
- **Sections:** current series → series archive grid (artwork cards) → filter or search by series, speaker, book of the Bible → live stream and podcast links.
- **Depth:** full archive. Captions and notes where available.

## Single sermon

- **Job:** watch and engage with one message.
- **Layout:** video first, then title, passage, speaker, date, notes link, a short summary, and the passage text (in the church's translation). Next and previous messages in the series.

## Ministries index ("Next Steps", "Get Involved")

- **Job:** help someone find where they fit.
- **Hero:** short typographic header with one photo.
- **Sections:** grouped by life stage or purpose (Grow, Serve, Care), each group as styled cards or an editorial index, with every ministry linking to its page.
- **Depth:** every ministry listed once, with a one-line description.

## Ministry page (Kids, Youth, Women, Men, Groups, Outreach)

- **Job:** answer "is this for me, and how do I start?"
- **Hero:** the ministry's own photo and sub-brand color or mark, inside the site's brand constants.
- **Sections:** who it is for (ages, stage) → when and where → what happens → leader (photo, name, contact) → upcoming events for this ministry → one next step (sign up, visit, contact).
- **Kids pages add:** safety and check-in, background checks, what to bring, pre-registration.

## Events

- **Job:** find something to attend and register.
- **Layout:** short header, filters by audience or ministry, then a list or grid with date, time, place, and audience visible without clicking. Featured events may get larger cards.
- **Single event:** date and time, place with map link, who it is for, cost, registration action, and a contact.

## Give

- **Job:** make giving simple and trustworthy.
- **Hero:** short, calm, with one primary "Give online" action visible immediately.
- **Sections:** why we give (short, with Scripture if desired) → other ways to give (text, mail, in person, stock) → where gifts go (a few plain numbers or stories, if the church shares them) → giving FAQ (receipts, recurring gifts, security).

## About / Beliefs ("Vision & Beliefs", "Who We Are")

- **Job:** build trust: who leads, what we believe, where we came from.
- **Sections:** mission and vision → what we believe (summarized, with a link to the full statement) → our story (history, in brief) → affiliation or denomination → leadership preview linking to the staff page.
- **Statement of faith:** set as readable sections with headings and Scripture references, not a single wall of text.

## Leadership & Staff

- **Job:** put faces and names to the church.
- **Layout:** pastors first with larger portraits and short bios, then staff and elders in a consistent grid (same photo crop, shape, and treatment for everyone), with roles and a contact method.

## Contact

- **Job:** reach a person.
- **Layout:** office hours, phone, email, and address as text, a map link, a short form (name, email, message, optional prayer-request checkbox), and expectations ("We reply within one business day").

## Livestream ("Watch Live")

- **Job:** join the service online.
- **Layout:** the player at the top, the next service time and a countdown or "starting soon" state, an order of worship or notes link, a connect card for online visitors, and a give link.

## Campus or location pages (multisite)

- **Job:** everything local for one campus.
- **Layout:** campus photo hero with times and address → campus pastor → what to expect at this campus → campus events → map and parking.

## Traditional / liturgical additions

- **Worship page:** service times as a table, the church year (current season and this Sunday), appointed readings, bulletin or order of worship, and a short guide to the liturgy for newcomers.
- **Sacraments and life events:** baptism, confirmation, marriage, funerals, each with a contact.
