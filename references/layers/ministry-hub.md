# Ministry Hub (layer)

A layer, not a direction. Add it to any direction when a church has many ministries (kids, youth, young adults, groups, women, men, recovery, care, outreach, serve teams) that each need their own identity without fracturing the main brand. Record it in `MINISTRY.md` as `Layer: ministry-hub`.

The direction still sets the fonts, colors, button shape, photo shape, overlay, and spacing. The layer adds a system for ministry sub-brands and a directory that helps people find where they fit.

## What the layer adds

- **Ministry colors.** One color per ministry, chosen to sit beside the direction's palette and pass 4.5:1 with white text. Keep the list in `MINISTRY.md`. Typical: a deep navy, an ink or near-black, an olive or forest, a brick or clay, a plum. Never bright or light colors that fail with white text.
- **Ministry marks.** Each ministry's own logo, or a small custom icon family drawn from the church's mark (see `iconography.md`). Used as oversized watermarks at 6 to 10% opacity, bleeding off a card edge, and small in the ministry page header. Never as bullets.
- **Ministry cards.** Solid ministry color, or a photo beside or under a ministry-color panel, in the direction's radius and photo shape. The eyebrow is the ministry name (so people scanning can find it), the headline is its purpose ("Connecting people. Activating faith."), then who it is for and when, then one action. The link's accessible name includes the ministry name ("Learn more about Youth"). All cards share type, padding, and button style.
- **Photos on ministry cards** use the direction's overlay treatment. A ministry color may replace the tint color only on that ministry's own card and page header, at the same strength, and only if the text passes.
- **Grouping.** On the Ministries page, group by life stage or purpose (Grow, Serve, Care; or Kids, Students, Adults, Everyone) with a short heading per group.
- **A moment where the identities show together.** Watermarks alone are too quiet to carry the layer. Give the index one place where every ministry's mark appears in its color at a readable size: a hero wall of colored tiles, a row of marks, or the group bar.
- **A group bar for many ministries.** With more than about 8, put a bar under the hero listing each group and its ministries as anchor links, so visitors can jump straight to theirs.
- **Ministry page headers.** Each ministry page opens with its color or photo, its mark, its name, who it is for, and when it meets (see `page-types.md`).

## Where it shows up

| Page | With the layer |
|---|---|
| Homepage | The "find your place" section may become a row of 3 to 5 ministry cards instead of an index, if the church prefers color over type. Keep the homepage limits. |
| Ministries index | The full grouped grid of ministry cards. This is the layer's main page. |
| Ministry pages | Sub-brand header in the ministry color with its mark; the rest of the page follows the direction. |
| Events | Audience tags and filters use the ministry colors as small dots or chips, never as full backgrounds. |

## Fitting it to each direction

- **Warm Editorial:** muted ministry colors (navy, olive, clay, plum), serif card headlines, leaf-shaped photo cards.
- **Traditional / Liturgical:** few colors (two or three deep tones), cards as solid panels with crisp corners, the parish mark or small icons instead of loud logos. Group by Worship, Formation, Fellowship, Outreach.
- **Bold Contemporary:** near-black and one deep color per ministry, heavy sans headlines, full-photo cards with strong scrims.
- **Playful Modern:** deep, saturated ministry colors with black text on any light tints, heavy lowercase headlines, a hand-drawn accent on one card at most.
- **Classic Confident:** navy-tinted photo cards or solid navy and accent cards, uppercase eyebrows, small-radius corners.

## Misses to avoid

- Ministry colors drift over time. Add a new ministry by choosing from the recorded list, not by inventing a new color.
- Watermarks above about 12% opacity compete with the headline. Check text contrast against the card color blended with the watermark, not the plain color; a watermark under the text can drop a pair below 4.5:1. Keep watermarks in the corner away from the text.
- Uneven group sizes leave holes in a grid. Lay out each group on its own: 2 cards as halves, 3 as thirds, 4 as a 2 by 2 or one wide photo card plus three, 5 as one wide card plus two, then two.
- Similar ministry colors side by side blur together (petrol beside navy, indigo beside violet). Neighboring cards should differ clearly in hue or lightness; rearrange or adjust one color.
- Do not give each ministry its own font. The shared fonts hold the hub together.
- A wall of identical cards is still a wall. Group them, vary photo and solid cards, and keep descriptions to one line.
