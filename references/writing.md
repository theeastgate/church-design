# Writing for the page

Design and words work together. These rules cover headlines, buttons, and short UI copy, not sermons or long articles.

## Voice

- Warm, plain, and specific. Write to one guest, in second person ("you").
- Say what you mean: "Sundays at 9 and 11" beats "Join us for worship experiences."
- Avoid vague phrases that mean nothing to a stranger: "authentic community," "doing life together," "a place to belong" (unless followed by specifics).
- Match the tradition's tone: playful churches can be casual; liturgical churches can be reverent. Both stay clear.

## Headlines

- Under about 10 words. One idea.
- Lead with the visitor's question or need: "There's a place for you here," "Every stage of life has a place in the church," "Serving our city and to the ends of the earth."
- Section headlines should make sense on their own when a visitor skims.

## Eyebrows

- One line, about five words or fewer ("Plan your visit," "This Sunday · November 1").
- Names the section or the ministry; the headline under it does the persuading.

## Buttons and links

- Verb first, two to four words: "Plan your visit," "Watch the latest sermon," "Give online," "Find a group."
- Be specific. Several "Learn more" buttons on one page are fine visually, but their accessible names must say what they are about.
- Primary actions use the same label everywhere ("Plan your visit" is not "Plan a trip" on another page).

## Church words

Explain on first use, or replace:

| Insider word | Plain option |
|---|---|
| Narthex, foyer | Lobby, main entrance |
| Fellowship hall | Gathering room, café |
| eGroups, LIFE groups, Rooted | Small groups (and keep the brand name as a label) |
| Rite I, Rite II | Traditional language service, contemporary language service |
| Eucharist, Communion, the Lord's Supper | Keep the tradition's word; add a short note for guests |
| Tithes and offerings | Giving |
| The 11:00 | The 11 AM service |

## Scripture

- Quote exactly in the configured translation, with reference and abbreviation.
- Keep homepage verses short (one or two verses).
- Do not attach a verse to a page just for decoration; it should fit the content.

## Placeholders

Use bracketed placeholders for unknown facts: `[Service times]`, `[Street address]`, `[Pastor name]`. Never invent them.
